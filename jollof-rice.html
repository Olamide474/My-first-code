Nigerian favourite

Jollof Rice

Ingredients

  • 2 cups parboiled rice
  • Tomatoes and peppers
  • Onion and garlic
  • Stock or water
  • Scotch bonnet pepper
  • Seasoning cubes and salt
  • Thyme, curry, and bay leaves
  • Vegetable oil

Cooking steps

  1. Blend the tomatoes, pepper, and onion until smooth.
  2. Fry the blended mixture in oil until it thickens and turns deep red.
  3. Add seasoning, spices, and a little stock to build the flavour.
  4. Stir in the rice and enough stock to cook it evenly.
  5. Cover and let it steam until the rice is tender and rich in flavour.
  6. Serve with salad, grilled chicken, plantain, or beans.
