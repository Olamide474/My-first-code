Grilled delight

Suya

Ingredients

  • Beef or chicken chunks
  • Ground pepper
  • Groundnut powder
  • Onion powder
  • Seasoning cubes and salt
  • Skewers
  • Oil or butter for basting

Cooking steps

  1. Season the meat with pepper, groundnut powder, onion powder, salt, and seasoning.
  2. Let it rest for at least 30 minutes so the flavour sinks in.
  3. Thread the meat onto skewers and grill or roast until cooked through.
  4. Baste lightly while cooking to keep it juicy.
  5. Serve with onions, fresh tomatoes, and cucumber.
