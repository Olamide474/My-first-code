Sweet snack

Puff-Puff

Ingredients

  • 2 cups flour
  • 1/2 cup sugar
  • 1 teaspoon yeast
  • 1 cup lukewarm water
  • Pinch of salt
  • Vegetable oil for frying

Cooking steps

  1. Mix the flour, sugar, yeast, and salt together in a bowl.
  2. Add warm water gradually until the batter is smooth and slightly thick.
  3. Cover and let it rise for 20 to 30 minutes.
  4. Heat oil until hot but not smoking.
  5. Scoop the batter into the oil and fry until golden brown.
  6. Drain on paper and serve warm.
