Hearty soup

Egusi Soup

Ingredients

  • Ground egusi
  • Meat, fish, or prawns
  • Spinach or bitter leaf
  • Palm oil
  • Onion and blended pepper
  • Seasoning cubes and salt
  • Crayfish and locust beans

Cooking steps

  1. Cook your meat, fish, or prawns with seasoning until tender.
  2. Heat palm oil and fry the onion and pepper until fragrant.
  3. Stir in the ground egusi and cook until it thickens.
  4. Add stock, crayfish, and locust beans to deepen the flavour.
  5. Simmer gently, then add spinach or bitter leaf at the end.
  6. Serve with pounded yam, fufu, or rice.
